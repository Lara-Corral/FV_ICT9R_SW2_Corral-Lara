OB Montessori Home Page

At Operation Brotherhood Montessori Center, every child is cared and supported throughtmethod of Dr. Maria Montessori. Welcome to the O.B. Montessori Center in the Philippines! We are a progressive pre-k to 12 co-educational school, providing a similar experience and being an alternative to international schools. At OBMC, we combine the aspects of life and tradition to guide a child way to life. Our mission is to help our students develop an outstanding and unique character, as well as independent leaders who strive, direct, and, in this evolving world, imrove the future and promote good. Explore our programs and discover the vibrant community that makes OBMC a unique place for a child to grow and develop.
